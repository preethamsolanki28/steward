# Steward: Problem, Track and Judging Fit

## 1. Problem statement

> Build an always-on, private assistant that works for you while keeping your data under your control. Give it persistent memory, reusable skills, access to the tools and information you choose, and the ability to carry out tasks across your daily workflows. Use at least one NVIDIA open source model, and use tools such as NVIDIA NemoClaw, OpenShell, Hermes Agent, and Nebius Serverless to bring personal AI to assemble, secure, and run your own personal AI system.

Source: the Personal AI Track text on Devpost. It was checked word for word against the Rules page on 2026-10-02.

## 5. Judging criteria and how the project addresses each

- **Stage One** is pass/fail.
- **Stage Two** uses 4 equally weighted criteria, each scored 1–5. The 1–5 scale comes from an organizer update on Devpost, not the Rules. Ties are broken in the order the criteria are listed below.
- **Organizer bar:** "a chatbot with a system prompt" is the floor. Judges want "an assistant that remembers context across sessions and takes real action on your behalf", and "Judges love to see tracks pushed further than the basics."

Measurable proof points:

- **DG-1 table** (T0.3; re-run through the full Shield in T2.5). It has 4 rows, {Lightning, Super} × {plain, pseudonymized}, each reporting success, placeholder integrity, p50 latency and tokens. PASS requires at least one model to reach all three: ≥8/10 runs with the right tools in order and valid JSON args, ≥95% of placeholders intact, and p50 ≤45 s. The table lives in the docs/02 decisions log and appears in the README and the video (4 s).
- **`make leak-test` passes**: 0 seeded identifiers across all recorded outbound payloads (T2.5). Its output goes in the README and the video (3 s).
- **`make egress-check`**: Hermes cannot reach example.com because it sits only on the `internal` network (M0 Done-when). Shown in the video (3 s).
- **DG-2** (T0.4): `MEMORY.md`/`USER.md` actually changes in ≥4/5 attempts. A pass unlocks the learned-memory beat; a fail switches to the edit path.
- **T3.5 E2E in CI**: the legit chase scores Low and approval produces exactly one send. The BEC request scores High ("lookalike of acme-logistics.example"), is rejected, and nothing is sent. A concurrent double-approve produces one send.
